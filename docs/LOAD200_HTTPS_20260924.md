# Isolated 200-player HTTPS stress test, 2026-09-24

## Verdict

**Overall FAIL under the existing HTTP P95 < 250 ms gate.** The 200-player
gameplay, reconnection, and per-player receipt/accounting checks passed. The
failed measurement was the `/guest/` HTTP P95 of 397.8 ms over the current
community-Wi-Fi-to-Singapore route. Do not convert this into a wedding-venue
network pass or a 200-physical-phone claim.

The generator used 200 Socket.IO guests at 10 taps/second/player during race
windows, with one staff control socket, through an IP-restricted isolated HTTPS
listener at `https://luckyhappy1009.com:8443`. It ran against an independent
copy of the newly deployed app at `127.0.0.1:3998`. Production port 3000,
production HTTPS port 443, and the production game were not reset.

Of 6,556 rejected taps, 4,826 occurred while teams were stunned; 910 had
stale state, 415 landed after a race window closed, and 405 hit the tap
cooldown. These were reported as rejections, not silently counted. The full
per-player ledger, all six 8-second windows, quiz summaries, and award payload
are in `reports/load200-https-20260924.json`.

## HTTP path diagnostic

After the game, 10 new and 10 keep-alive requests each to `/guest/` and
`/healthz` were sent at no more than one request per second from community
Wi-Fi. All returned HTTP 200. Cold `/guest/` total P50/P95 was 321.1/391.3
ms; reused connections typically took 90-97 ms. Cold `/healthz` P50/P95 was
305.5/366.6 ms. New connections spent roughly 99 ms in TCP and 111 ms in TLS
at the median, before the remaining round trip. The full timings are in
`reports/load200-http-path-20260924.json`.

On the VPS after the game, direct loopback `/guest/` requests took roughly
1.6-2.3 ms. Loopback through the isolated HTTPS listener with a new TLS
connection took roughly 40-55 ms. These comparisons support an access-path
component to the cold-load delay, but do not identify a specific router, ISP
hop, or server region as the sole cause. No CDN/cache setting was changed.

## Scope and cleanup

This was a simulator test, not 200 physical devices on the wedding venue
network. It did not measure real phone rendering, first-load assets under
200 independent browsers, projector FPS, audio, or venue Wi-Fi capacity.
Those still require the planned rehearsal. The isolated Node and Caddy units
were stopped; TCP 8443 access was removed; ports 3998, 8443 and 2020 had no
listeners. Production Node and Caddy remained active with a healthy empty
`LOBBY` and zero sockets.
